# Code of Conduct

TraceFox expects contributors to collaborate in a respectful, technical, and constructive way.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in technical discussions, reviews, and issue reports.
- Focus on the code, data, and reproducible behavior instead of personal attacks.
- Assume good intent, but be direct about bugs, regressions, and design risks.
- Share enough context when reporting problems so others can reproduce them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions away from the engineering work.
- Bad-faith reports, reviews, or contribution attempts.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and discussions that violate this code of conduct.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
